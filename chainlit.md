# Auto Parts Advisor 🚗🔧

Describe your vehicle issue, trip, or part need in everyday language, and I’ll search the inventory for relevant products.

Example questions:

- “My brakes are squealing — what do I need?”
- “I’m doing a winter road trip. What should I pick up?”
- “I have a sedan.”

I’ll recommend matching parts from the catalog with price, stock status, compatibility, and reasoning.
